Klassrun Technologies Ltd

We designed, built and delivered the official website of Ibeju-Lekki Local Government Area, Lagos State.

What we delivered

The council’s previous website ran on an ageing platform with no working administrator access. Eight years of communications were trapped inside it.

We recovered that archive in full, rebuilt the site from scratch, and handed the council a system its own staff run without a developer.

The work

  • Recovered eight years of council communications from a database with no administrator access, restoring every photograph at full resolution
  • Rebuilt the site on Next.js and TypeScript, designed mobile-first for the networks residents actually use
  • Built a content management system council staff operate unaided, with publishing, leadership records and service pages all under their control
  • Delivered search, push notifications, offline support, structured data and automated weekly backups
  • Migrated the site onto the official government domain and handed over every account to the council
